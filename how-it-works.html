How a crittr order reaches a customer's house

Two companies, two different jobs. That's the whole thing.

Once — setting up the stock

Every order — automatic

  1. Customer buys. Clicks Add to cart on crittr.ai. Pays $39.99 — one price, postage already in it, nothing to work out.
  2. Stripe takes the payment and collects the delivery address. The money lands in your account.
  3. crittr emails KAK automatically. "Send one jar of CRITTR Joint to this address." No action from you.
  4. KAK posts it. Picks a jar, boxes it, hands it to USPS. $2.75 plus postage at cost.
  5. Customer gets a tracking email and the box arrives.
The only manual step in the entire flow. Someone types the tracking number into /admin/fulfilments — that's what sends the customer their "on its way" email. Ask KAK to email tracking back and it's ten seconds an order, or hand it to a VA.

Product cost is the only estimate here — everything else is a published rate. A real quote moves the bottom line by about $500 per $1 of product cost.

Lead with Joint. Not because the margin per jar is higher, but because it is taken daily — a 60-count jar lasts a 40lb dog about a month, so customers reorder monthly. Calm is situational (fireworks, travel) and a jar lasts months. Over a year that's roughly $200 per customer versus $34.

Sales tax, in one line

It is never your money. The customer pays it, Stripe calculates it by destination, and you pass it to the state once a quarter. Stock sitting in a Texas warehouse means you register in Texas and charge Texas customers only — everyone else pays none until you sell over $100,000 into their state.

What's already built

All of the software exists and is live on crittr.ai:

  • One-price checkout with Stripe Tax by destination
  • Paid order automatically split into purchase orders and emailed to the warehouse
  • Despatch desk at /admin/fulfilments for tracking numbers
  • Customer tracking emails and an order status page
  • A product cannot be sold unless it has a supplier — so "we took the money and nothing shipped" is impossible by design
  • CRITTR Joint is listed at $39.99 and deliberately not purchasable until stock exists
